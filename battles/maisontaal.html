The Twelfth Battle of La Maisontaal

While Mallobaude seeks a crown, Arkhan seeks something buried beneath the abbey. Two battles decide whether the road to Couronne passes through its crypts.

Arkhan’s prize

The Rebel advance toward Couronne has reached the opportunity Arkhan wanted. Maisontaal Abbey holds Alakanash, the Great Staff of Nagash. Arkhan is an ancient lich devoted to restoring the Great Necromancer, and the staff matters far more to him than who wears Bretonnia’s crown. He persuades Mallobaude to divert the army from its march on the capital.

The abbey is no unprepared shrine. After an earlier undead assault, Tancred I strengthened its defences. Theodoric of Brionne now leads its defenders as a penitent, refusing to abandon this charge for the wider civil war. Heinrich Kemmler, the Lichemeister, accompanies the attackers, but his interests and Arkhan’s will not remain aligned once Nagash’s staff is within reach.

The detour Arkhan wanted

Enter from The Road to Couronne only if the Rebels have conquered Parravon and choose this detour. They may make that choice once. Carry forward the campaign's character availability, army restrictions, rewards, Kingdom Without a King and Plentiful Ranks.

Arkhan's true prize is not Bretonnia. For centuries he has planned to recover an artefact of Nagash from La Maisontaal. First Maldred and now Mallobaude have served his purpose: plunge the country into war, then exploit the disorder to reach the abbey.

La Maisontaal is no undefended sanctuary. After the great attack thirty years earlier, Tancred I fortified it. Its barracks house archers and men-at-arms from fourteen duchies, led by nobles who consider the posting an honour. Theodoric of Brionne, seeking penance for his unchivalrous conduct, has restored drills, patrols and observation posts. His determination has protected the abbey while preventing its garrison from helping Louen elsewhere.

Now Theodoric sees a chance for redemption. He orders the defenders into the pastures south of the abbey and calls on them to rekindle Bretonnia's hope. Arkhan's long-prepared assault is about to begin.

Deployment maps

Table one: the abbey

Battlefield and deployment

The Undead attack and Bretonnia defends. Place terrain normally. Place a Fortified Manor at the indicated defender-edge position if available. Its buildings are Haunted Mansions and its enclosed grounds are Charnel Pits, using the rules below.

  1. The defender deploys first in the mapped zone, more than 12″ from the centre point of its own table edge.
  2. The attacker deploys second. Cavalry, Monstrous Cavalry and models with Fly use the flank zones; everything else uses the central battering-ram zone.
  3. No attacker may deploy within 12″ of a defender.

The attacker takes the first turn. Play eight game turns unless an instant victory ends the battle.

The Sisters and Dark Enchantments

The Three Sisters are Unbreakable. While at least one survives, every other defender unit is Stubborn.

At the start of each game turn, both sides roll off for Dark Enchantments. The attacker adds 4. The defender adds 3 if all three Sisters are within 3″ of one another; 2 if only two are within 3″; 1 if no pair is within 3″ but at least one Sister remains on the battlefield; or 0 if none remain. If the modified totals tie, both players reroll, keeping their modifiers, until there is a winner.

  • If the attacker wins, every attacking unit gains D6 Wounds, following Resurrecting Fallen Warriors.
  • If the defender wins and at least one Sister survives, the attacker places the small round template anywhere. Each Sister beneath it suffers a separately rolled D3 Wounds, with no saves.

Winning this table

The attacker wins immediately if its General is within 12″ of the centre of the defender's table edge at the end of any Bretonnian turn. Otherwise determine the table's result using ordinary victory points. Agree who is General before play.

Table two: Theodoric's Last Charge

Bretonnia attacks and the Undead defend. Use the second deployment map. Roll off to choose table halves before alternating deployment. Place terrain normally. Alternate deployment normally, but Bretonnia alternates individual units between its two corner zones. For grouped character or war-machine placements, alternate their individual models between those zones too.

Roll off for first turn, adding 1 for the side that finished deployment first. Play five game turns unless an instant victory ends the battle.

Original character rules

The official Arkhan, Mortarch of Sacrament, is free and excluded from the Lords percentage. If present, he must be General and cannot cast spells; other Wizards may cast normally. Theodoric has Hatred specifically against Arkhan, and his weapons gain Armour Piercing. Apply your agreed character ruling; these Arkhan-specific benefits and restrictions do not automatically transfer to Mallobaude.

The massed lances

Knights Errant and Knights of the Realm may have unrestricted unit size and form Lances up to four models wide. Those units gain Stomp only when they have more than 15 models, successfully charged that turn and are in Lance formation.

Winning this table

  • Bretonnia wins immediately when the defending General is removed as a casualty.
  • The Undead win immediately when Theodoric is removed as a casualty after he has inflicted at least one Wound on the defending General.
  • Otherwise use ordinary victory points.

Removing Theodoric before he has wounded the General does not satisfy the printed Undead instant-win condition.

Run the two battles together

  1. Begin the abbey battle alone. Complete its first whole game turn.
  2. Start Last Charge. Thereafter begin the tables' game turns together, waiting as necessary: abbey turn 2 accompanies Last Charge turn 1.
  3. If the Undead General in Last Charge becomes a casualty, the abbey attacker loses its Dark Enchantments bonus thereafter.
  4. An instant victory ends that table. Victorious units may transfer to the other table as reinforcements in the following game turn.

Use your agreed entry edges. This transfer does not use Quenelles's 50% quota, turn-three timing or 5+ arrival roll. Keep casualties as they stand unless your agreed ruling specifies otherwise.

Core reinforcement arrival

Face the arriving unit directly onto the battlefield with its entire rear rank touching the agreed entry edge. The official update requires every model to be within twice its Movement of the edge. It cannot charge or march that turn, otherwise acts normally, and counts as moving when shooting. The placement limit is not permission to march.

Rules shared by both tables

Death on the Winds

Reduce casting values of spells from Death, Undeath, Vampires and Nehekhara by 1, to a minimum of 3+.

Undeath Ascendant

Undead and Nehekharan Undead suffer one fewer Wound from Unstable or the death of their General or Hierophant. This is cumulative with Undead Battle Standards and Animated Construct.

Resurrecting Fallen Warriors

  1. Restore the champion first, then the musician, displacing rank-and-file as necessary. Do not restore the standard bearer.
  2. Heal existing multi-Wound rank-and-file models, including command models.
  3. Restore rank-and-file models. Fully heal each restored multi-Wound model before adding another.

Add restored models to the front rank until it contains at least five models, or three for Monstrous Infantry, Monstrous Beasts, Monstrous Cavalry and Chariots. Further models may be added to the front or rear. If the unit already has multiple ranks, add restored models only to the rear rank. Do not exceed the unit's starting size.

Healing spells and magic items normally cannot heal characters or their mounts; a character does not share healing applied to a joined unit. Units with Animated Construct, Vampiric, Ethereal or Large Target recover at most one lost Wound per successful healing spell or item. Agree how those spell/item limits apply to Dark Enchantments before play.

Abbey terrain

  • Charnel Pit: all units within 6″ suffer −1 Leadership; Undead within 6″ gain Regeneration (6+).
  • Haunted Mansion: at the end of the Shooting phase, all units within 6″ suffer D6 Strength 1 hits without armour saves. A unit inside causes Fear.

Ordinary victory-point fallback

Where a table calls for ordinary victory points, count destroyed enemy units or those that fled the table at full cost. Add 100 for an enemy General slain or fled from the table, 25 for a qualifying captured standard or 100 for the battle standard, and 50 when a unit champion kills an enemy character in a challenge. Standards qualify when removed through Last Stand or slain in close combat, not through shooting, magic or other non-combat losses. Both character and mount must be destroyed to claim their points; created units normally award none.

Under official update v1.8, wiping out the opponent or leading by at least 100 victory points wins; otherwise draw. A points victory is crushing if the winner also scores at least twice the opponent's total. This fallback does not replace either table's instant victories or the episode's combined-result table.

Pregame decisions

Before selecting armies, agree which characters lead each table and how the Arkhan-specific rules apply after the characters change tables. Also agree reinforcement entry edges, healing of living Mousillon troops, and the route after a draw or an objective victory that does not open the abbey.

Keep these rulings with your campaign record. Full questions and conflicting instructions.

Into the crypts, or back to the road

The episode's winner receives one Victor's Reward. The guide does not award a separate campaign reward for each table.

  • Rebel victory: proceed to The Duel for Alakanash, using your agreed ruling for the doorway objective and surviving characters.
  • Loyalist victory: Mallobaude abandons La Maisontaal and resumes his advance; return to The Road to Couronne.
  • Draw: use your agreed onward route.

Record the combined result, whether the abbey's instant objective was met, character casualties, reward and the spent one-use detour.
