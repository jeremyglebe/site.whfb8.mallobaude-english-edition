4 Person Suggested Campaign

Four recurring commanders, guest roles when their forces are absent, and a personal victory record separate from the struggle for Bretonnia.

Choose your commander

Bretonnia

Louen, the loyal dukes and the crown’s defenders. Leads Loyalist campaign decisions and chooses their rewards.

Mousillon

Mallobaude, the renegade dukes and the armies loyal to him, including undead when permitted. Leads Rebel campaign decisions and chooses their rewards.

Asrai

First choice of Wood Elves and forest spirits: Drycha’s Host at Châlons, then the Asrai alliance when it joins. These are distinct narrative forces, not a single permanent allegiance.

Arkhan

Arkhan and his undead contingent, usually alongside Mallobaude. Commands other undead or guest armies when Arkhan is absent. The player’s label does not make Arkhan himself legal in every scenario.

These are player identities and first-choice commands, not four independent campaign states. When an army is missing, take the guest assignment below. A player’s score follows the player; their army’s relics do not.

For a shared army, keep the scenario’s total points and build one legal force before dividing its units. Share its normal General, army-selection limits, magic pool and other allowances; do not duplicate them for a second commander. Each commander controls an agreed group of units. The army’s lead commander settles shared decisions. Change the division before each battle so neither player is left with only a token command.

Two separate results

The story

Use each scenario’s normal victory conditions and campaign consequences. Record duchies, defections, rewards, abductions, deaths and the Revelation. The final battle determines the narrative ending, not the players’ cumulative scores.

The players

  • One personal victory for winning a battle. Both commanders of a winning shared army earn one. Record draws and losses separately; neither adds a victory.
  • Guest victories count normally. Arkhan winning with Kairos scores a personal victory, not a Rebel victory in the civil war.
  • Châlons has a separate Asrai objective in the suggested variant below. Successful escape with Morgiana earns Asrai one victory, independently of the Bretonnian battlefield result.
  • Linked games: at Quenelles each flank player scores their own flank result; all four then score the central battle. At Maisontaal use the combined episode result, not an extra award for each table.
  • Every scheduled scoring game should give all four a command. Agree the number of repeatable engagements in advance. Do not schedule extra scoring games for only two players.
  • Most victories wins the player competition. Equal totals are joint winners. Record attendance; if someone misses games, agree a catch-up or a different scoring basis before continuing rather than quietly changing the system afterward.

Do not add individual kill counts or raw army victory points across scenarios. Unequal armies and special objectives make them poor measures of a player’s campaign performance.

Relics belong to armies, not players

  • Keep one reward ledger for each campaign army or named recipient specified by the reward. Bretonnia and Mousillon lead their sides’ choices; other commanders can advise.
  • A shared army receives only the reward the scenario awards, not one copy per human commander.
  • Guest commands receive no imported relics. When Arkhan plays Kairos, none of Arkhan’s collected items or rewards comes with him. The same applies when Asrai commands Ku’gath or Bretonnian troops.
  • Campaign rewards gained while commanding a guest force go to the source’s specified narrative recipient. A personal victory does not redirect them to the player’s usual army.
  • After a list change, use only rewards and bearers the campaign permits. Neither the Revelation nor a player swap grants a free transfer between living Rebels, Arkhan and the Asrai.

Who plays each battle?

Follow the campaign’s branches, not this list as a compulsory schedule. After Quenelles, the advance toward Couronne and the pursuit toward Mousillon are alternative routes.

Three rival armies, not two teams. Keep Mallobaude at 50% and each Daemon army at 100% of the agreed budget. There is one turn/card per army, not per person. Arkhan’s flank; then surviving reinforcements and a share of the Rebel centre.

Suggested pairings: Arkhan versus Asrai on one table; Mousillon versus Bretonnia on the other. Assign Orion and Ariel to different Loyalist flanks. Play both flanks, then the central battle with the permitted survivors. Keep the source’s total army split, not three full armies per side. If Revelation has not happened, play only Father and Son: Bretonnia + Asrai as Loyalists versus Mousillon + Arkhan as Rebels, all with the legal Bretonnian forces.

Châlons: an independent Wildwood Host

Suggested house rule. Agree this before playing. The printed scenario assigns the Host to the Loyalist player and allows Rebel control; it does not give Drycha a separate player victory.

  • Asrai builds and commands the Host throughout. Keep its 50% budget, Drycha requirement, Coeddil exclusion, turn-3 arrival and arrival distances.
  • The Host acts once per game turn, during the Loyalist turn’s normal phase sequence; it receives no additional army turn or extra magic pool. Before deployment, agree how Bretonnia and Asrai divide that turn’s shared power and dispel dice. Resolve any disputed order of actions before rolling dice.
  • Replace the Rebel 5+ temporary-control roll and permanent control transfer after abduction. The Asrai player makes the Host’s decisions instead and may treat either Bretonnian force as an enemy once the Host enters.
  • Keep the source’s Morgiana proximity, carrying-unit escape and recovery procedures. Keep the required separation from Rebels after abduction; that does not transfer command to Mousillon.
  • Asrai earns one personal victory if the Host escapes with Morgiana. Merely picking her up does not suffice. Otherwise record an Asrai loss, independently of the battlefield winner.
  • Bretonnia and Mousillon still compete for the battlefield result. Arkhan shares Mousillon’s result. Morgiana’s actual fate determines her campaign availability, whether or not her abductors win the battlefield.
  • Score Host casualties separately: a Bretonnian side earns the normal points only for Host units it destroys. The Host does not contribute its kills to either Bretonnian side. This is part of the variant, not a resolution supplied by the original.

This gives Asrai a reason to pursue Drycha’s goal, but no scoring scheme can erase friendship or future alliances. Play the assigned role in good faith; do not trade help in this battle for favors in a later one.

Before the first game

  1. Assign the four labels and agree who can lend models for guest armies.
  2. Adopt the scoring and Châlons variant above, or write down the replacements before anyone chooses a list.
  3. Record each battle’s army divisions and the player controlling every named character. Do not field a character twice across linked forces.
  4. Keep a personal W–D–L tally beside the campaign’s separate reward and story ledger.

Playing the Campaign describes the original two-camp framework and its allowance for allied players. This page is a suggested four-person arrangement; the battle entries retain the source scenario rules and their campaign branches.
