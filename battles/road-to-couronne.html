After Quenelles

Enter this stage after Mallobaude wins Quenelles, or return here after the Maisontaal episode or the duel for Alakanash directs you back. Do not take this route after Louen wins Quenelles: that result leads to The Flight of the Serpent.

No one can agree on Louen’s fate. Some say seven sisters carried him to Silverspire for the Lady to heal; others say he lies buried above Quenelles. Worse rumours make him a puppet of Mallobaude’s necromancers. Without their king, the dukes hesitate to help their neighbours while their own lands burn. Southern duchies fall and Mallobaude marches north.

The Serpent’s confidence becomes recklessness. At Gisoreux, Adelaix, Montfort and other battlefields, he challenges every knight willing to face him. In the campaign’s account he emerges unharmed; resolve your engagements below.

This stage is optional. If skipped, count Mallobaude as having won one victory. Agree the onward route before skipping: Maisontaal has its own optional Parravon requirement. Rules notes.

Choose the next duchy

The Rebel player chooses a remaining Loyalist duchy. Already defeated duchies cannot be chosen. Couronne cannot be the first target; it becomes available on later engagements. After each battle, repeat this stage; choosing an adjacent duchy is recommended, but not required.

If the Rebels have conquered Parravon, they may make a detour to The Twelfth Battle of La Maisontaal. This choice may be made only once.

When Couronne is chosen, proceed to The Battle of Couronne. A crushing Loyalist victory forces that choice as described below.

Forces and the cost of losing the king

  • The Rebel army must include Vampire Mallobaude.
  • The Loyalist army must include the Loyalist duke of the chosen duchy. Use the updated duke roster, respecting deaths and absence/substitution notes.
  • Use the Mousillon army against Bretonnia. The Asrai have withdrawn and Bretonnian Civil War no longer applies after Revelation.
  • Louen cannot be used for the rest of the campaign.

Kingdom Without a King

Loyalists suffer −1 Leadership for Break tests, not for all Leadership tests or Panic tests.

Plentiful Ranks

Every Mousillon army may add one Zombie unit worth at most 200 points per Necromancer or Master Necromancer, including Arkhan and Kemmler. Agree whether these units cost points and count toward army-selection limits. Rules notes.

Keep unit origins and any Red Duke interlude restriction on vampire numbers. Named characters lost at Maisontaal or in the Alakanash duel remain unavailable according to those outcomes. Army lists and profiles remain in the rules reference.

Fight a Pitched Battle

Use the Battleline setup below; no special points ratio or first-turn exception applies. Rules notes.

Ordinary Battleline setup

  1. Agree equal points. Place at least D6+4 terrain pieces, alternating placement after a roll-off.
  2. Roll off for choice of table half. Deploy in opposing zones more than 12″ from the centre line.
  3. Roll off separately for first placement and alternate deploying units. All war machines form one placement; all characters deploy last as one placement.
  4. Roll off for first turn, adding +1 for the player who finished deployment first.
  5. Play six game turns or an agreed time limit.

Core victory-point procedure

Score the full cost of enemy units destroyed or fled from the table. Add 100 points if the enemy general is slain or has fled the table, 25 per captured standard or 100 for the battle standard, and 50 when a unit champion kills an enemy character in a challenge. A standard qualifies when removed through Last Stand or slain in close combat, not through shooting, magic or another non-combat loss. Both a character and its mount must be destroyed to claim their points. Created units normally award none.

Apply the official v1.8 correction: wiping out the opposing army or leading by at least 100 victory points wins. Otherwise the result is a draw. A points victory is crushing if the winner also scores at least twice the opponent's total. Scenario rules can replace ordinary duration or victory conditions.

Core rulebook pp. 142–144; official update v1.8.

Resolve the result

  • The winner receives one Victor's Reward, using the eligible reward categories for their army.
  • If the Rebels win, Mallobaude attempts to corrupt the duke under the roster's procedure: corruptible dukes join him; absolutely loyal dukes refuse and are executed. The duchy is conquered and its troops become available.
  • If the Loyalists win a crushing victory, Mallobaude must hasten the end of the war: the Rebel player must choose Couronne as the next duchy to conquer.

A normal Loyalist victory does not have that compulsory effect. The stage supplies no additional draw consequence. After the battle, repeat this stage unless the route sends you to Maisontaal or Couronne. Record rewards, conquered duchies, dukes' fates and whether the one-use detour has been spent.
