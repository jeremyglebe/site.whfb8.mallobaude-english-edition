Campaign stage 10 · the kingless route

The Battle of Couronne

The last loyal dukes stand before the gates of Couronne. Mallobaude demands another duel, but the champion who answers is no mortal knight.

The kingdom’s last defender

Mallobaude has reached Couronne after defeating his father Louen at Quenelles. With the king absent, the capital is the final obstacle between the Serpent and the throne. His campaign against the Lady’s order has become an openly undead conquest, and the surviving Loyalists must make their stand without Louen.

Their champion is Sacremor, the Green Knight, Bretonnia’s legendary supernatural protector returned from Lacrimora. To the defenders he offers a last hope that the realm has not been abandoned. To Mallobaude he represents the very chivalric faith the rebellion set out to destroy. Their confrontation can decide the battle and the kingdom’s future.

The Serpent at the gates

Enter this battle when Couronne is chosen on The Road to Couronne. A crushing Loyalist victory there forces Mallobaude to make Couronne his next target.

Mallobaude has fought his way north, defeating the champions who dared to meet him. At Couronne he finds the surviving loyal dukes united, their banners side by side. Their depleted army is badly outnumbered, but Sacremor, the Green Knight, has returned from Lacrimora to defend his people.

The Serpent issues his customary challenge. This time Sacremor accepts. In the remembered account, the Green Knight cuts Mallobaude down before he can escape, and the rebel army breaks. The traitor's body is burned and his ashes scattered. Fight the duel to determine how your campaign ends.

Agree before play

Prepare the siege terrain and agree what happens if both duelists survive or die simultaneously. The scenario and campaign guide give different endings when both survive; see the rules notes.

Choose the armies

Agree a battle size. The Loyalists receive that many points; the Rebels receive 150% of it. For a 2,000-point battle, the Rebel army therefore has 3,000 points.

  • Rebels: use the Mousillon army and include Mallobaude, Vampire Duke of Mousillon.
  • Loyalists: use Warhammer Armies: Bretonnia and include the Green Knight, Sacremor.

Carry forward eligible rewards, duke availability, unit origins and any Red Duke restriction on vampire numbers. Louen remains unavailable and the Asrai have withdrawn.

Kingdom Without a King

The Loyalists suffer −1 Leadership for Break tests, not for all Leadership tests or Panic tests.

Plentiful Ranks

The Mousillon army may add one Zombie unit worth at most 200 points per Necromancer or Master Necromancer, including Arkhan and Kemmler. Use your agreed treatment of payment and army-selection accounting.

Prepare the siege

Set up the battlefield as a siege and deploy the armies in the usual order. Prepare the terrain, deployment zones and applicable siege rules together before selecting deployment positions. Keep the centre available for the duel.

Do not deploy Mallobaude or Sacremor with their armies. Place the two at the centre of the table in base contact with one another.

The Loyalists take the first turn. Play six turns, unless a duelist's death ends the battle earlier.

Duel to the death

  • In the first round of close combat, both duelists count as having charged.
  • Both are Unbreakable for the duration of the duel.
  • No other unit may intervene. Neither duelist can be charged or targeted by shooting, spells or other attacks.

Resolve their combat using their respective profiles and equipment, with these scenario rules.

Determine the winner

If Mallobaude or Sacremor dies, the battle ends immediately and his opponent is declared the winner.

If neither has died by the end of turn six, the scenario calls for ordinary victory points. If the result is tied, the Rebels win through their numerical superiority. Use the campaign-ending agreement made before play if both survive.

Ordinary victory points

Score the full cost of enemy units destroyed or fled from the table. Add 100 points if the enemy general is slain or has fled the table, 25 per captured standard or 100 for the battle standard, and 50 when a unit champion kills an enemy character in a challenge. A standard qualifies when removed through Last Stand or slain in close combat, not through shooting, magic or another non-combat loss. Both a character and its mount must be destroyed to claim their points. Created units normally award none.

Under the corrected core rule, wiping out the opposing army or leading by at least 100 victory points wins. Otherwise the ordinary result is a draw. A points victory is crushing if the winner also scores at least twice the opponent's total. Couronne's special ending and tie rule take precedence as agreed before play.

The fate of Bretonnia

  • If Mallobaude dies, he is unavailable for the rest of the End Times.
  • If Sacremor is killed, he returns to life as usual.
  • A Loyalist victory leads to Conclusion I: The Coronation of Gilles.
  • A Mallobaude victory leads to Conclusion III: The Vampire King.

The campaign guide instead calls the campaign a draw if both duelists survive and asks the players to write their own conclusion. Apply the ending you agreed before the battle rather than deciding it after seeing the victory points.
