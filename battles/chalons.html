How you arrive here

After the Year of Woe, the campaign enters The Revolt. Its first attack cannot target Aquitaine, Couronne or Quenelles. The winner of each opening battle chooses the next dukedom; once Aquitaine is selected, play Châlons. The guide suggests two or three engagements first to preserve the story's shape, but does not make that a fixed count.

The Revolt is optional. If skipped, the guide says to count Mallobaude as having won one battle before moving to this stage. Bring forward your actual record of rewards, conquered dukedoms and dukes' loyalties, rather than assuming that every game followed the narrative.

Armand's defiance

Louen has ordered Bretonnia's armies to unite against the threat from Mousillon. Duke Armand of Aquitaine rides out instead, trusting his own force and Morgiana the Fay Enchantress to confront Mallobaude at the edge of the Forest of Châlons.

The Wildwood Host emerges as an apparent ally of the Loyalists, but its purpose is not Armand's victory. Drycha intends to seize Morgiana. The source story carries the Fay into Mannfred's hands and ultimately into the preparations for Nagash's return; that future is not forced upon your game.

Two results matter independently: who wins the battlefield, and whether Drycha escapes with Morgiana. The abduction can change which side controls the forest spirits without changing the victory conditions.

Choose the forces

Agree a points value. Build the three forces below as percentages of that value. The Loyalist player prepares both Armand's force and the Wildwood Host.

Set the battlefield

Use numerous woods to represent the Forest of Châlons. Deploy only Mallobaude's and Armand's armies in the opposing zones shown; leave the Wildwood Host off the table.

The Rebel player takes the first turn. The battle lasts six turns.

The forest's betrayal

The Briarmaven of Woe

  1. At the beginning of game turn 3, bring the Wildwood Host onto the table through the woods.
  2. Place each Host unit within 3″ of a wood and more than 6″ from an enemy unit. The Host may act normally.
  3. At the start of each Rebel turn, the Rebel player rolls a D6. On a 5+, they may use unengaged Spirits as though they belonged to their army. Engaged Spirits are unaffected and continue fighting, regardless of their enemy.

Mannfred's Spoils

  1. If a Host unit comes closer than 3″ to Morgiana, it abducts her. Remove her from the table.
  2. The entire Host passes to the Rebel player's control. Any Host unit fighting Rebels immediately ends that combat and is separated from its enemy by 1″.
  3. If the carrying unit reaches a table edge or enters a Forest, it escapes with Morgiana, who is permanently captured.
  4. If that unit is destroyed before escaping, return Morgiana to the table less than 3″ from the unit's last position.

The French source uses “less than 3″” for the abduction and her return, and a maximum of 3″ for the Host's arrival. Those distinctions are retained here.

Bretonnian Civil War

Apply the Civil War rules to the Bretonnian forces only. The forest Spirits are unaffected. The source-compared common procedure and the relevant tables are reproduced below so the scenario does not send you to another chapter for them.

Civil War

Note: These rules are adapted from the Civil War rules published in the French monthly edition of White Dwarf, issues 213-216, between January and April 2012. Only the rules needed for The Revolt of Mallobaude campaign are reproduced here.

For this campaign, ignore the Faction rule from the original expansion. It was intended to make armies more thematic, but does not suit the structure of this campaign.

In a Civil War, armies chosen from the same army book clash. Because both sides use many of the same rules, Jervis Johnson created a set of simple tables to add uncertainty and variety to the battle. These rules do not alter army selection, so no advance preparation is required.

Before the battle, roll once on two of the following tables. The first table is used by every army. The other two are specific to Bretonnia and Vampire Counts; the campaign or scenario rules specify which one to use.

If the Battle Standard Bearer is slain, every unit in that army must take a Panic test.

Before the battle, a hero from each army is chosen to represent their side in a duel of honour.

Before the game begins, each player nominates one Lord or Hero. Place the two characters in base contact at the centre of the battlefield; they fight normally in the next Close Combat phase. No other model may interfere, and both characters are Unbreakable. The victor causes Terror for the rest of the battle.

Valour

Every knight is determined to enter into legend.

Whenever a character slays an enemy in a challenge, compare their points values. If the slain model was worth more points than the victor, the victor's side scores 100 Victory Points. If the battle does not use Victory Points, take comfort that the hero's deed will be celebrated throughout the land!

The Jacquerie

The peasants rise in revolt and refuse to bend to the nobility's will.

Units with the Peasant's Duty special rule cannot use the Leadership of a nearby knight, but gain Frenzy and Stubborn.

11

Lances of Heroes

The blood of Gilles le Breton runs in the veins of his sons!

Knights of any type re-roll To Wound rolls of 1 in a turn in which they charge.

12

The Grail

Place a marker representing the Grail at the centre of the battlefield. A Knight unit or character with any vow other than the Grail Vow that moves into contact with the marker must stop and drink. Take a Leadership test: if failed, the unit or character has Stupidity for the rest of the battle; if passed, replace its current vow with the Grail Vow. Only one unit or character may drink each turn. If an enemy unit is in contact with the Grail, it must first be destroyed or made to flee.

Resolve the battle and the abduction

Determine the winner using normal Pitched Battle victory conditions. Drycha's betrayal and Morgiana's abduction do not themselves change the scoring. Use the corrected core scoring summary below.

Campaign consequences

  • The winner receives one Victor's Reward.
  • If Drycha successfully abducts Morgiana, the Fay cannot be used in Bretonnian armies for the rest of this fan campaign, or later End Times campaigns until a possible rescue.
  • If the abduction fails, every subsequent army containing Morgiana must also include Grail Knights. She must join that unit and may not leave it.
  • The campaign's final conclusion may change her ultimate fate.

Next: Stage 4 — The Betrayal.

Record the relics, Mallobaude's victory or defeat here, and Morgiana's capture separately: each affects how many dukes Mallobaude can turn in the next stage. A successful abduction is not a substitute for winning the battle.

Core victory-point procedure

Score the full cost of enemy units destroyed or fled from the table. Add 100 points if the enemy general is slain or has fled the table, 25 per captured standard or 100 for the battle standard, and 50 when a unit champion kills an enemy character in a challenge. A standard qualifies when removed through Last Stand or slain in close combat, not through shooting, magic or another non-combat loss. Both a character and its mount must be destroyed to claim their points. Created units normally award none.

Apply the official v1.8 correction: wiping out the opposing army or leading by at least 100 victory points wins. A points victory is crushing if the winner also scores at least twice the opponent's total. Otherwise the result is a draw. Scenario rules and applicable Civil War results can replace ordinary duration or victory conditions.

Source: core rulebook, printed pp. 142–144, with official update v1.8, PDF pp. 2 and 6. This includes the corrected victory threshold, not the obsolete threshold in the uncorrected scan.

Châlons changes army budgets, deployment, turn order and arrivals: do not replace these with Battleline setup. The source does not settle victory-point attribution when Wildwood units change sides.
