When the campaign reaches Quenelles

Arrive after The Conquest, either when its winner chooses Quenelles as the next target or when that optional stage is skipped. Carry forward Revelation, army eligibility, surviving dukes, rewards and any Red Duke interlude outcome.

In the campaign's account, the Lady calls dispossessed knights to Quenelles through voices in the wind and running water. Louen's army stands beside Orion's host as Mallobaude advances from the west among the Black Grail Knights. An arrow loosed toward the Serpent signals the battle. Father and son meet in single combat; Mallobaude casts Louen down, Bretonnian courage fails, and the elves are left covering the retreat.

At the forest’s edge Ariel collapses, stricken by a mysterious poison. Orion rushes to help her and the Asrai withdraw. That is the campaign’s historical outcome; your battle result determines the next stage. Retain Morgiana’s actual fate from your earlier games.

Which version do you play?

Mallobaude is already revealed

Play two flank battles, then the central Father and Son battle. Rebels use the Mousillon Army; Loyalists use the Alliance Against Undeath. Agree army points beforehand; large forces are recommended because each army will be divided.

Split each army into three forces. No force may exceed the combined points of the other two: no force can be more than half its army, but equal thirds are not required. Each named character may appear in only one initial force. Surviving characters can subsequently transfer as reinforcements.

Mallobaude is still unrevealed

Play Father and Son only. Both armies use Bretonnia. Use mortal Mallobaude, not his vampire profile. Do not play the flanks or use the central reinforcement procedure. Louen and Tancred remain required. Apply the mortal-army Panic consequence instead of the undead consequence.

Agree before play: whether Bretonnian Civil War rules apply after Revelation. The guide and scenario give conflicting instructions. Rules notes.

The two flank battles

Use this section only on the revealed route. One Rebel force must include Arkhan; the other must include Kemmler and Krell. Rebel dukes are recommended, not compulsory. One Loyalist force must include Orion; the other must include Ariel. Agree which Rebel force faces each Loyalist force.

Use identical tables with Battleline setup below. Add more forests to Ariel’s deployment half near Athel Loren. Rules notes.

  • In Orion's battle, the Loyalists take the first turn.
  • In Ariel's battle, the Rebels take the first turn.
  • Each battle lasts six turns and uses ordinary Pitched Battle victory conditions.

Choose survivors for the centre

After each flank battle, its winner may select 50% of their units remaining on that table as reinforcements. Count units, not points or models. Round fractions up under the core rulebook's division rule: five surviving units therefore permit three reinforcement units.

Record each reinforcement unit’s originating flank, surviving models and remaining wounds. Agree how draws and attached characters count toward the quota. Rules notes.

Father and Son

The revealed central Rebel force must contain Vampire Mallobaude. The Loyalist central force must contain Louen and Tancred. On the unrevealed route, substitute mortal Mallobaude and Bretonnian army lists as described above.

Use ordinary Pitched Battle setup. The Rebels take the first turn; play six turns and use ordinary Pitched Battle victory conditions. The revealed route adds the reinforcements below.

The leaders' confrontation

Louen and Mallobaude each gain 1″ of additional movement when moving toward the other by the most direct route. This does not compel movement or a charge, and the scenario does not expressly change their Movement characteristic. Both Mallobaude profiles independently require him to issue and accept challenges when possible; use the appropriate linked profile's other challenge rules.

The throne is at stake

  • If Louen is defeated in combat, the Bretonnians immediately take a Panic test, then test again at the start of each of their turns for the rest of the battle.
  • If Vampire Mallobaude is defeated, the undead army immediately takes an “Instability test,” repeating at the start of each of its turns.
  • If mortal Mallobaude is defeated, use Panic for his army in the same manner as the Louen procedure.

Agree before play: whether “defeated” means slain, broken or beaten in a combat round. Louen’s Panic effect applies to Bretonnians, not the Asrai, and does not cause automatic flight. Rules notes.

Undead army: agree an Instability procedure before playing the revealed route. The scenario does not define its test; neither core Unstable nor Daemonic Instability supplies this rule. Rules notes.

Reinforcements from the flanks

Revealed route only. Begin with the central forces deployed. From turn three onward, during each active player's Remaining Moves phase, roll one D6 for each of their pending reinforcement units. On 5+, it may enter. Continue rolling for pending units on later friendly turns; there is no automatic final-turn arrival.

When the first reinforcement arrives, choose a short table edge. Units from that flank enter through this edge; units from the other flank enter from the opposite short edge. Retain the origin record even if different sides won the two flanks.

Under core Reinforcements, face the unit directly onto the battlefield with its entire rear rank touching the entry edge. The official update also requires every model to be within twice its Movement of that edge. This placement limit does not grant a march: the unit cannot charge or march that turn, otherwise acts normally, and counts as moving for shooting.

Do not restore lost models or wounds between battles. Once-per-battle abilities and special rules recharge; Dragon Breath is the source's example. This is not explicit permission to replenish every expended magic item.

Core setup and scoring

Use the scenario's fixed first-turn rules instead of the ordinary first-turn roll-off below. Scenario instructions and any applicable Civil War results override the baseline.

Ordinary Battleline setup

  1. Agree equal points. Place at least D6+4 terrain pieces, alternating placement after a roll-off.
  2. Roll off for choice of table half. Deploy in opposing zones more than 12″ from the centre line.
  3. Roll off separately for first placement and alternate deploying units. All war machines form one placement; all characters deploy last as one placement.
  4. Roll off for first turn, adding +1 for the player who finished deployment first.
  5. Play six game turns or an agreed time limit, unless a Civil War result changes this.

Core victory-point procedure

Score the full cost of enemy units destroyed or fled from the table. Add 100 points if the enemy general is slain or has fled the table, 25 per captured standard or 100 for the battle standard, and 50 when a unit champion kills an enemy character in a challenge. A standard qualifies when removed through Last Stand or slain in close combat, not through shooting, magic or another non-combat loss. Both a character and its mount must be destroyed to claim their points. Created units normally award none.

Apply the official v1.8 correction: wiping out the opposing army or leading by at least 100 victory points wins. Otherwise the result is a draw. A points victory is crushing if the winner also scores at least twice the opponent's total. Scenario rules and applicable Civil War results can replace ordinary duration or victory conditions.

Core rulebook pp. 142–144; official update v1.8.

Civil War procedure and tables

Note: These rules are adapted from the Civil War rules published in the French monthly edition of White Dwarf, issues 213-216, between January and April 2012. Only the rules needed for The Revolt of Mallobaude campaign are reproduced here.

For this campaign, ignore the Faction rule from the original expansion. It was intended to make armies more thematic, but does not suit the structure of this campaign.

In a Civil War, armies chosen from the same army book clash. Because both sides use many of the same rules, Jervis Johnson created a set of simple tables to add uncertainty and variety to the battle. These rules do not alter army selection, so no advance preparation is required.

Before the battle, roll once on two of the following tables. The first table is used by every army. The other two are specific to Bretonnia and Vampire Counts; the campaign or scenario rules specify which one to use.

2D6Civil War Battle Table
2

Annihilation

The only way this dispute will be resolved is by the complete destruction of the other side.

All units in both armies are Unbreakable. Ignore the normal rules for Game Length and Victory Conditions. Instead, the game lasts until one side is completely destroyed; the surviving side wins.

3

Lust for Battle

The troops in both armies are desperate to get to grips with each other.

All units in both armies have the Vanguard special rule.

4

Pent-Up Fury

The two sides are seething with pent-up aggression.

All units in both armies have the Devastating Charge special rule.

5

Undeserving

Each side considers the other unworthy to carry the battle standards under which they march.

The Victory Points for captured standards increase to 100 points per standard bearer and 500 points for a Battle Standard Bearer.

6

We Will Not Yield

Neither side is willing to back away from this fight.

All units in both armies are Stubborn.

7

Ancient Enmity

The two sides have harboured numerous grudges against each other for centuries.

Roll twice on this table, re-rolling any further rolls of 7 or duplicate results.

8

Hated Rivals

The two generals despise each other.

Each Army General Hates the opposing Army General and wins the battle immediately if that rival is slain. If both Generals are slain at exactly the same time, the battle is a draw.

9

Bloody Feud

The two sides have been feuding for years.

All units in both armies Hate all units in the other army.

10

Unreasoning Spite

Both sides are so blinded by hate that they have lost all reason.

All units in both armies are subject to Frenzy.

11

We Shall Not Yield

Both rival armies believe completely in the justness of their cause.

All units in both armies are Immune to Psychology.

12

Guerrilla War

The two sides have been ravaging each other's lands in a vicious guerrilla war.

Up to three units in each army may have the Ambushers special rule if their commander wishes.

2D6Bretonnian Civil War Table
2

Favours of the Lady

Will the Lady favour her sons?

The first time a unit is called upon to make a ward save granted by the Blessing of the Lady, it must also take a Leadership test. If the test is passed, all ward saves the unit receives from the Blessing are improved by 1.

3

Thoroughbreds

All Warhorses, Pegasi, and Royal Pegasi have +1 Strength.

4

As a Token of My Esteem

A handkerchief, scarf, or ribbon tied around a knight's lance or armour may have miraculous effects.

Each player may choose one magic item from the Bretonnian magic items list and give it to a Hero or Lord in their army, following the normal rules for magic items. If the bearer is slain in close combat by another character, the slayer automatically takes the item.

5

The True King

Claimants to the throne are all too common, and only the army's great standard proves the General's right to command.

If the Battle Standard Bearer is slain, every unit in that army must take a Panic test.

6

Duel to the Death

Before the battle, a hero from each army is chosen to represent their side in a duel of honour.

Before the game begins, each player nominates one Lord or Hero. Place the two characters in base contact at the centre of the battlefield; they fight normally in the next Close Combat phase. No other model may interfere, and both characters are Unbreakable. The victor causes Terror for the rest of the battle.

7

Ancient Enmity

The two sides have harboured numerous grudges against each other for centuries.

Roll twice on this table, re-rolling any further rolls of 7 or duplicate results.

8

Valour

Every knight is determined to enter into legend.

Whenever a character slays an enemy in a challenge, compare their points values. If the slain model was worth more points than the victor, the victor's side scores 100 Victory Points. If the battle does not use Victory Points, take comfort that the hero's deed will be celebrated throughout the land!

9

The Jacquerie

The peasants rise in revolt and refuse to bend to the nobility's will.

Units with the Peasant's Duty special rule cannot use the Leadership of a nearby knight, but gain Frenzy and Stubborn.

10

For the Glory of the Damsels

Magic floods the realm, invigorating the powers of its Damsels and Prophetesses.

Whenever a Damsel or Prophetess casts a spell, add D6 to the casting total. This additional die can cause a miscast as normal.

11

Lances of Heroes

The blood of Gilles le Breton runs in the veins of his sons!

Knights of any type re-roll To Wound rolls of 1 in a turn in which they charge.

12

The Grail

Place a marker representing the Grail at the centre of the battlefield. A Knight unit or character with any vow other than the Grail Vow that moves into contact with the marker must stop and drink. Take a Leadership test: if failed, the unit or character has Stupidity for the rest of the battle; if passed, replace its current vow with the Grail Vow. Only one unit or character may drink each turn. If an enemy unit is in contact with the Grail, it must first be destroyed or made to flee.

The campaign turns

The winner receives one Victor's Reward. No separate campaign reward per flank is stated.

  • If the Asrai participated, Ariel is poisoned afterward and they withdraw. Loyalists return to Bretonnia.
  • If Mallobaude is still unrevealed, he reveals himself after the battle. Apply The Revelation. Revelation occurring only after Quenelles does not bring the Asrai into the war.
  • Mallobaude wins: apply Kingdom Without a King below, then continue to The Road to Couronne.
  • Louen wins: continue to The Flight of the Serpent.

Draw: agree the next campaign stage before play. Rules notes.

Kingdom Without a King

Louen becomes unavailable for the rest of the campaign. Loyalists suffer −1 Leadership for Break tests, not all Leadership tests or Panic tests.

For the rest of the campaign, every Mousillon army gains Plentiful Ranks: one Zombie unit worth at most 200 points per Necromancer or Master Necromancer, including Arkhan and Kemmler. Agree whether these units cost points and count toward army-selection limits. Rules notes.
