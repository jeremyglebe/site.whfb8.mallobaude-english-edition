From Quenelles toward Mousillon

Enter this stage when Louen wins Quenelles. Apply that battle's aftermath first: Mallobaude reveals himself if he has not already, and any participating Asrai withdraw. This is not the Road to Couronne branch that follows Mallobaude's victory.

Mallobaude retreats to his stronghold. Beneath Mousillon wait creatures drawn there by centuries of corruption; he intends to unleash them on his pursuers. But defeat has shaken the dukes who joined his bid for the throne. Each day brings new desertions.

Louen is determined to finish the war. He has lost time trying to keep the elves in the alliance, but they place their stricken queen before the human conflict. He gathers his battered army and advances through duchies held by Mallobaude's supporters. If the elves never intervened, omit their part in the withdrawal.

Before playing, settle any unavailable-leader or desertion questions in the rules notes.

Optional battles, mandatory campaign changes

Apply the defeat-related changes below before deciding whether to skip this stage. Once they are in effect, the pursuit battles are optional. If skipped, count Louen as having won one victory and proceed to The Siege of Mousillon. Do not assign a particular defeated duchy, captured duke or reward to that skipped victory.

Unreliable allies

These rules apply at every battle through the end of the campaign, including the subsequent siege. Keep each Bretonnian unit's duchy of origin visible, as required when Mallobaude revealed himself.

Before deployment: honour regained

Roll one D6 for each Bretonnian unit in the Mousillon army. On a 6, it passes to the Loyalist player's control. If its corresponding duke is dead or captured, it changes sides on 5+ instead. Units from the duchy of Mousillon are unaffected.

Use the updated duke record before rolling: a duke captured in an earlier pursuit battle changes the threshold for that duchy's units in later battles. Agree how attached characters and deployment placement will be handled when a unit changes sides.

After deployment: the wind changes

Roll one D6 for each necromancer in the army. On a 6, that necromancer deserts: remove him as a casualty. Arkhan and Heinrich Kemmler are exempt.

Choose where the pursuit catches up

The Loyalist player chooses the duchy in which the pursuit battle takes place. Ideally choose one adjacent to the previous battlefield; adjacency is a recommendation, not a requirement. Mousillon cannot be chosen first.

After each engagement, repeat this stage, again ideally choosing an adjacent duchy. When Mousillon is selected, proceed to The Siege of Mousillon. A crushing Rebel victory can accelerate that transition under the outcome rule below.

Choose armies from the current campaign state

Use the post-Revelation Mousillon army and Bretonnia. The Asrai have withdrawn; Bretonnian Civil War is no longer used. Preserve character availability, earned rewards, unit origins and any Red Duke interlude restriction.

No named character is compulsory for these Pitched Battles.

Do not apply Kingdom Without a King or Plentiful Ranks on this route: those follow Mallobaude's victory at Quenelles, not Louen's.

Fight a Pitched Battle

Use the Battleline setup below. Resolve Bretonnian defections before deployment and necromancer desertions afterward.

Ordinary Battleline setup

  1. Agree equal points. Place at least D6+4 terrain pieces, alternating placement after a roll-off.
  2. Roll off for choice of table half. Deploy in opposing zones more than 12″ from the centre line.
  3. Roll off separately for first placement and alternate deploying units. All war machines form one placement; all characters deploy last as one placement.
  4. Roll off for first turn, adding +1 for the player who finished deployment first.
  5. Play six game turns or an agreed time limit.

Core victory-point procedure

Score the full cost of enemy units destroyed or fled from the table. Add 100 points if the enemy general is slain or has fled the table, 25 per captured standard or 100 for the battle standard, and 50 when a unit champion kills an enemy character in a challenge. A standard qualifies when removed through Last Stand or slain in close combat, not through shooting, magic or another non-combat loss. Both a character and its mount must be destroyed to claim their points. Created units normally award none.

Apply the official v1.8 correction: wiping out the opposing army or leading by at least 100 victory points wins. Otherwise the result is a draw. A points victory is crushing if the winner also scores at least twice the opponent's total. Scenario rules can replace ordinary duration or victory conditions.

Source: core rulebook, printed pp. 142–144, with official update v1.8, PDF pp. 2 and 6.

Resolve the pursuit

  • The winner receives one Victor's Reward.
  • If the Loyalists win in a rebel duchy, its corresponding duke is captured or killed and cannot be used for the rest of the End Times. This affects the Mousillon army's composition and the future defection threshold for units from that duchy.
  • If the Rebels win a crushing victory, the Rebel player may accelerate the campaign by choosing Mousillon as the next duchy attacked.

The Rebel option is permissive, not compulsory. It requires a crushing victory, not an ordinary win. A Loyalist victory outside a rebel duchy does not trigger the stated duke-removal consequence.

Agree any additional campaign consequences for a draw before play. Record rewards, duke availability, unit defections and necromancer losses, then repeat the pursuit unless Mousillon has been selected.

When the chosen duchy is Mousillon, continue to Stage 12: The Siege of Mousillon. Keep Unreliable Allies in effect.
