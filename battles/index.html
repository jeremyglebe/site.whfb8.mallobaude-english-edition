Campaign at the table

Battles

The story, forces, battlefield, rules and consequences of each engagement, brought together in the order you play them.

Working draft · not yet a complete campaign

This is a consolidated play document, not a replacement for the faithful translations. Each battle identifies its sources and separates the historical narrative from the outcome of your game. The eventual sequence will connect the fan campaign with official End Times scenarios; no new connection or alternate outcome has been imposed here.

Optional prologue · Stage 1

The Year of Woe

Daemonic Assault on Mousillon. Mallobaude's sortie collides with Kairos's assault and Ku'gath's intervention. Three armies fight for very different victories.

Campaign story, scenario rules, deployment and the relic reward are gathered here. Verified multiplayer, Chaos Ascendant and fortress procedures are included. Army profiles and a few source ambiguities remain explicit dependencies.

Optional opening engagements · Stage 2

The Revolt

Choose the first target, fight the early clashes and resolve which dukes turn or die. The allegiance roster, Civil War tables and eligible rewards sit with the battle sequence.

Stage 3 · after the opening revolt

The Battle of Châlons

Armand confronts Mallobaude, while Drycha's apparent aid conceals her purpose: abducting the Fay Enchantress. Campaign approach, battle rules, Civil War tables and both kinds of aftermath are gathered here.

The Revolt entry explains the intervening engagements; this is not a direct jump from Mousillon to Châlons.

After the prologue

Both playing and skipping the Year of Woe lead to The Revolt. The Revolt and Châlons drafts keep that sequence explicit, without skipping the intervening stage.

For stages not yet consolidated, the existing campaign guide remains the reference for those stages. Nothing on this page replaces the campaign's branching decisions.
