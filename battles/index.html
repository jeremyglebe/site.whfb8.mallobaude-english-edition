Campaign at the table

Battles

The story, forces, battlefield, rules and consequences of each engagement, brought together in the order you play them.

Start with the Year of Woe or skip to the Revolt. Follow each battle’s outcome to the next stage, carrying forward rewards, conquered duchies and the fates of the dukes.

Optional prologue · Stage 1

The Year of Woe

Daemonic Assault on Mousillon. Mallobaude's sortie collides with Kairos's assault and Ku'gath's intervention. Three armies fight for very different victories.

Three armies, an optional fortress and the relics that can sway the dukes of Bretonnia.

Optional opening engagements · Stage 2

The Revolt

Choose the first target, fight the early clashes and resolve which dukes turn or die. The allegiance roster, Civil War tables and eligible rewards sit with the battle sequence.

Stage 3 · after the opening revolt

The Battle of Châlons

Armand confronts Mallobaude, while Drycha's apparent aid conceals her purpose: abducting the Fay Enchantress. Campaign approach, battle rules, Civil War tables and both kinds of aftermath are gathered here.

Fight or skip the opening Revolt engagements before reaching Châlons.

Stage 4 · the crown's counterattack

The Betrayal

Use the relics, Châlons result and Morgiana's fate to determine defections, then play Louen's response. Campaign consequences, Civil War tables and core procedure are gathered together.

Stage 5 · optional engagements

The Conquest

The counterattack spreads across Bretonnia. Choose targets, resolve dukes' fates and track the Revelation that brings the dead and the Asrai into the war.

Stage 6 · the campaign divides

The Battle of Quenelles

Father and son meet in one battle or three linked engagements. Surviving troops from the flanks may reinforce the centre.

Stage 7 · optional engagements

The Road to Couronne

Track the kingless duchies, Mallobaude's northward advance and the optional Maisontaal detour.

Following the campaign

Both playing and skipping the Year of Woe lead to The Revolt, then Châlons. Optional engagements can shorten the campaign.

For later stages, follow the campaign guide. Consult rules notes before play for interpretations your group needs to settle.
