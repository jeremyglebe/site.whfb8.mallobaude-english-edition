Before the revolt

Mallobaude is preparing to challenge the crown when the Year of Woe interrupts his plans. Across Bretonnia, Kairos Fateweaver's Daemons hunt the twelve enchanted artefacts once borne by Gilles le Breton's Companions. Each recovered relic strengthens the invaders. By the twelfth month, only one remains beyond their reach: the relic in Mousillon.

The siege threatens more than Mallobaude's rebellion. Arkhan, who intends to use the civil war to advance Nagash's return, fears that Chaos is deliberately frustrating his work. Mallobaude must repel the Daemons and keep them out of the castle's keep.

At the height of the assault, the Bretonnians launch their sortie. Nurgle has no intention of letting Tzeentch destroy a city so dear to him and sends Ku'gath's legions to intervene. Mallobaude's men know nothing of that purpose. They see Daemons fighting Daemons and give quarter to neither.

This is the opening battle. There is no previous campaign result to carry into it. The fan campaign makes this prologue optional. If you skip it, count it as a Mallobaude victory and apply the relic reward below before beginning the Revolt.

The account in the campaign

In the campaign’s account, Ku’gath strikes down Kairos, then falls to some ten blessed lances. Both Daemonic armies vanish, leaving their relics to Mallobaude. He displays them to lend legitimacy to his bid for power. Your battle’s winner and relic award are determined below.

Prepare the battlefield

Arrange the deployment zones as shown. Place terrain outside the fortress normally, but leave the Bretonnian deployment zone clear: it is the open ground in front of the castle.

Deployment order

  1. Mallobaude deploys first. War machines must be inside the fortress. Missile units may be inside it. All other units, including Mallobaude, must deploy outside.
  2. Deploy Kairos's army.
  3. Deploy Ku'gath's army.

Without a fortress: agree where war machines deploy before play. The optional-fortress note and deployment instructions conflict. Rules notes.

Play the battle

Turn order and duration

The battle lasts six turns. In the first turn, play in this order: Kairos, Ku'gath, Mallobaude. In later turns, use the shuffled-player-card procedure below.

Last Hope

Mallobaude's sortie leaves the fortress gates open. They cannot be closed.

Multiplayer turns: Trust No One

From turn two, shuffle one identifiable card for each player face-down. Reveal a card and complete that player's turn, then reveal the next. Once everyone has acted, reshuffle for the next game turn.

At the start of each phase, the active player chooses one opponent as the enemy. All other armies are neutral for that phase. Choose again next phase. Neutral units neither act nor suffer attacks or effects, including templates, scattered attacks and effects applying to “any unit.” They do not contribute to combat resolution, disruption, pursuit or overruns. Fleeing through them works as fleeing through enemies.

  • Movement: charge only the chosen enemy. Neutral units are impassable.
  • Magic: only the chosen enemy receives dispel dice. Offensive spells target that army; augments target your own. Neutral units ignore ongoing spell effects triggered this phase, and ongoing spells on neutral units cannot be dispelled this phase.
  • Shooting: shoot only the chosen enemy, not its units fighting neutrals.
  • Combat: if you are fighting only one opposing army, you must choose it. If fighting several, choose one and call truces with the rest. Move neutral units touching yours 1″ away by the shortest route, keeping their facing. If blocked or engaged with another army, leave them in place but exclude them from the phase.

For multiplayer roll-offs, everyone rolls. The highest roller determines the order for everyone involved; only tied highest rollers reroll.

Use Triumph & Treachery’s Trust No One multiplayer procedure below, with this scenario’s scoring. Do not use Victory Tokens, Treachery Cards or Mercenaries.

Chaos Ascendant: Tzeentch

The scenario fixes Tzeentch as ascendant rather than asking players to choose the ascendant god.

  • Replace Daemonic Instability with Unbreakable and Unstable. Daemons of the ascendant god omit Unstable: Tzeentch Daemons are therefore Unbreakable without Unstable; Nurgle Daemons have both.
  • Champions bearing the ascendant god's mark may reroll any dice when rolling on Eye of the Gods.
  • Storm of Chaos: when Reign of Chaos applies and Winds of Magic are doubles, the Chaos player may substitute the summoning result. If more than two dice determine the winds, examine the highest two. Substituted results summon Pink Horrors while Tzeentch is ascendant. An actual double six retains the choice of Bloodletters, Pink Horrors, Plaguebearers or Daemonettes.

Summon Infernal Legion

All Chaos Wizards additionally know this spell, with no lore attribute. Its range is 12″. Casting values of 14+, 17+ or 20+ summon a Daemonic unit worth up to 100, 150 or 200 points, respectively, chosen from the Legions of Chaos army list and belonging to the ascendant god.

Buy permitted upgrades and meet minimum unit size. Place the unit wholly within range and at least 1″ from units, buildings and impassable terrain. No line of sight or forward arc is required. Choose a legal facing and formation, with at least five models in the front rank when the unit contains more than five models. It cannot subsequently be dispelled and awards no victory points. If placement is impossible, it does not appear.

The Reign of Chaos summoning-table result instead creates 2D6+3 daemon infantry, with no upgrades or victory points. Place them anywhere on the battlefield (not restricted to the spell’s 12″ range), at least 1″ from units, buildings and impassable terrain, with a front rank of at least five. They do not appear without space or models.

Summoning: agree whether Ku’gath’s Nurgle-only restriction also limits summoned units. Rules notes.

If you use the fortress

Use ordinary building rules for each wall, gate and tower as a separate building, with the following exceptions from Blood in the Badlands.

  • Splitting Up: at deployment, divide a defending unit among sections if desired. Each portion becomes a separate unit for the entire battle and cannot recombine. Distribute command models among the portions.
  • Any unit type may garrison a section. A garrison may move into an empty connected section or attack an occupied connected section in the Combat phase. Winning captures it; losing leaves the attacker in its original section. Friendly units in adjacent sections may exchange places.
  • A tower or gate may contain a war machine and a unit. Measure the machine's attacks from its actual position. Enemy shooting chooses between it and the other garrison. Crew may join the defensive fighting party. Capturing the section destroys the machine.
  • Units may pass through a gate held by their own side as clear ground, and charge enemies beyond as though visible. This scenario additionally keeps the gates permanently open.
  • Up to ten models plus a war machine may shoot from each section.
  • External attackers are assumed to have climbing equipment. Unless flying, halve their Weapon Skill and Initiative, rounding down. Attacks from siege towers or into breached sections avoid this penalty.
  • Defenders gain +1 combat resolution per connected section occupied by a friendly unit. Other modifiers normally excluded from building assaults remain excluded.
  • A section can be assaulted by one unit from outside and one from each connected section. The fighting party remains capped at ten models per side, drawn from participating units.
  • A defender losing combat in a breached section must leave even without breaking. It may retreat into an adjacent empty section; if unable to leave, it is destroyed. The attacker occupies the section. Fortress sections are breached rather than destroyed, including by building-removal effects.

Use the fortress procedures above without starvation, relief forces, preliminary bombardment, siege budgets or the Badlands five-turn victory conditions. Agree how passage through an open, enemy-held gate works before play. Rules notes.

Determine the victor

Score the opposing generals slain, rather than using ordinary victory points first.

Scenario victory points
ArmyScoring
Kairos3 points if his army kills Mallobaude; 1 if it kills Ku'gath.
Ku'gath3 points if his army kills Kairos; 1 if it kills Mallobaude.
Mallobaude1 point if Kairos is slain, plus 1 more if Mallobaude's army delivers the killing blow. Score Ku'gath in the same way.

Each army can score 0–4 points. The highest score wins. Break ties using ordinary victory points.

Calculate the tie-break

Score the full cost of enemy units destroyed or fled from the table. Add 100 points if the enemy general is slain or has fled the table, 25 per captured standard or 100 for the battle standard, and 50 when a unit champion kills an enemy character in a challenge. A standard qualifies when removed through Last Stand or slain in close combat, not through shooting, magic or another non-combat loss. Both a character and its mount must be destroyed to claim their points. Created units normally award none.

Compare these totals only to break a tie in this scenario’s special scoring. Do not import the ordinary 100-point victory threshold here.

Carry the result forward

If Mallobaude wins

He gathers the Relics of the Companions. The Rebel player receives four Ancient Relic results from the Victor's Rewards table, usable at any time during the campaign.

Ancient Relic (Common): Before deployment, give one of your characters a magic item worth up to 75 points at no cost. Rewards may be combined; once used, a reward is spent and cannot be used in another battle. Keep track of the four rewards separately.

Rewards: agree whether the four Ancient Relics replace the normal Victor’s Reward and whether a Daemon winner receives a campaign reward. Rules notes.

If either Daemon army wins

The Rebels receive no relic award. Record the battle result and continue to the Revolt; agree how any general’s death affects the continuing story.

If you skip this battle

Treat the stage as a Mallobaude victory. Apply the corresponding relic outcome and continue.
