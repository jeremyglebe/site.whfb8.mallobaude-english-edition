Campaign stage 9 · after La Maisontaal

The Duel for Alakanash

Below the abbey, Arkhan discovers that Kemmler has betrayed him. Their armies clash among the fallen masonry for the Great Staff of Nagash.

Two masters of the dead

The fighting at Maisontaal has opened the way to Nagash’s relic. Alakanash, the Great Necromancer’s staff, is the prize Arkhan has sought through his involvement in Mallobaude’s rebellion. Recovering it serves Nagash, not merely the Rebel army’s immediate needs.

Heinrich Kemmler now turns against him. The Lichemeister has embraced Chaos and will not allow Arkhan to complete his purpose unopposed. Their confrontation in the abbey’s crypt is a struggle between rival loyalties within the invading force, not another battle between Louen and Mallobaude. Its survivor and the fate of the staff will change who returns to the campaign; the war for Couronne continues whichever necromancer prevails.

From the abbey to its crypt

After the Rebels win at La Maisontaal, Arkhan follows Kemmler into the abbey. Carry forward character casualties from that battle.

Before playing

Agree which Arkhan profile to use, percentage accounting for the free leaders, and how to proceed if either leader is unavailable or the Rebels won without forcing the abbey doors. Settle the chest interactions and non-death campaign outcomes listed in the rules notes.

Two servants, two masters

In the source’s narrated encounter, Arkhan notices Kemmler's absence as the abbey's defences collapse. The trail leads through scorched corpses to the crypt, where broken seals and dead knights mark the Lichemeister's passage. Kemmler turns on his former ally with Alakanash in his hands.

He accuses Nagash of manipulating him for decades without granting the promised power. Chaos, he claims, restored his knowledge and exposed the deception. He now serves the ruinous powers rather than the resurrection of the Great Necromancer. Arkhan answers by raising the bodies strewn around the chamber.

For this battle, Alakanash begins hidden in one of the three chests. Neither leader starts with it.

Choose the two armies

Agree army sizes of at most 500 points each. Both armies use Vampire Counts, not the Mousillon list.

  • Add Heinrich Kemmler to the first army free of charge.
  • Add Arkhan the Black to the second army free of charge.

Use the Arkhan profile and free-character accounting agreed before the battle.

Apply Civil War (Vampire Counts), using the procedure and tables below. You may also agree to use Patrols. Do not add Death on the Winds or Undeath Ascendant to this battle.

Prepare the crypt

  1. Use a 60 × 60 cm battlefield with a flat floor and fallen masonry from the abbey's collapsed roof.
  2. Place the stairway door on one side. Place one chest at the midpoint of each of the other three sides.
  3. Deploy Kemmler's army first, centrally, at least 5″ from every chest.
  4. Deploy Arkhan's army from the door, no closer to a chest than Kemmler's army and at least 4″ from Kemmler's army. Kemmler's army must advance enough to leave room.
  5. Begin play only after both armies are completely deployed. This is deployment, not an extra movement turn.

Kemmler takes the first turn. The baseline game lasts five turns, unless a Civil War result changes the duration or victory conditions.

The Crypt of Secrets

A unit that reaches a chest opens it. Resolve chests in the order they are first opened:

Chest openedRoll and contents
FirstRoll a D6. On 5+, it contains Alakanash; otherwise it contains an Anneau de Feu Maudit.
SecondRoll a D6. On 4+, it contains Alakanash; otherwise it contains a Sword of Might.
ThirdIt necessarily contains Alakanash.

Only Arkhan or Kemmler may carry Alakanash. The character must personally reach the chest containing it to pick it up. He may then move while carrying it. Alakanash grants no additional combat or spell powers in this scenario.

Win the duel

  • If Kemmler or Arkhan is killed, his opponent wins immediately.
  • If either character carries Alakanash to the crypt door, he wins immediately.
  • Otherwise, the character possessing Alakanash at the end wins.
  • If neither has found Alakanash by the end of turn five, the result is a draw.

Apply any Civil War result that overrides these conditions. Agree beforehand how to resolve a found but unclaimed staff or simultaneous deaths, unless a table result supplies the answer.

Civil War: procedure and tables

Before the battle, roll once on the common Civil War table and once on the Vampire Counts table. Ignore the original expansion's Faction rule, as instructed by this campaign.

Annihilation replaces the normal game length and victory conditions; Hated Rivals allows a draw if both generals die simultaneously. Apply the table results, including free units, extra spells and characteristic changes, after selecting the armies.

Record what actually happened

The campaign's consequences are triggered by death, not simply by winning the objective.

If Kemmler dies

  • Kemmler is unavailable for the rest of the End Times.
  • Arkhan takes Alakanash and remains with Mallobaude until the campaign ends.
  • Krell becomes Arkhan's bodyguard.

If Arkhan dies

  • Arkhan is unavailable unless Nagash resurrects him in a later campaign.
  • Another disciple takes over his later relic-search role. Zacharias the Everliving is one possible successor.
  • Kemmler flees to the Chaos Wastes. He can no longer be used in an Undead army; add him instead to Warriors of Chaos and later Legions of Chaos. His new prestige earns him a place among Archaon's lieutenants.
  • Kemmler takes Alakanash into the Realm of Chaos, where it is lost forever; Nagash must make do with a lesser staff on his resurrection.
  • Krell refuses to follow Kemmler and stays with Mallobaude.

If both leaders survive, both die, or the battle is drawn, use your agreed campaign resolution. Do not apply a death outcome to a leader who survived.

After the duel, return to The Road to Couronne, preserving the actual character and staff outcomes.
