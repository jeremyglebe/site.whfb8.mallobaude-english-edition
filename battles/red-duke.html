The Red Duke

Not every vampire in Bretonnia will bow to Mallobaude. The Red Duke pursues his own ambitions, and Arkhan moves to remove the rival threatening his plans.

An interruption, not a replacement

At any time after The Revelation, the Loyalist player may trigger the Red Duke's betrayal. Play it between two other battles. It does not replace the current campaign stage; record where you paused and return there afterward.

The episode is optional. If you skip it, count Arkhan as the victor and the Red Duke's lieutenants as killed. Apply the corresponding outcome below rather than treating the skipped episode as if nothing happened.

A rival in the shadows

The Red Duke of Aquitaine has no intention of swearing allegiance to a usurper. Bretonnia's disorder offers opportunities for his own designs, but his interference threatens Arkhan's work. In the campaign's account, Arkhan destroys the vampire's retinue, including the Dark Knight and the Banshee, while the Red Duke escapes. Years later, he reappears in Sylvania in Nagash's service.

Your result may differ: a Red Duke victory draws vampires away from Mallobaude, although the duke still leaves Bretonnia.

Choose the scenario and armies

Choose a scenario yourselves. Use its points limit, deployment, first turn, duration and objectives, then apply the character requirements and Civil War rules here.

  • Mallobaude's Rebel army must include Arkhan the Black. After Revelation, the Rebel army uses the Mousillon list.
  • The opposing force is not a Loyalist Bretonnian army. Use a Vampire Counts army containing the Red Duke, the Banshee and the Dark Knight.
  • Apply Civil War (Vampire Counts), using the common and Vampire Counts tables below. Use these tables rather than the Bretonnian Civil War tables.

Carry forward campaign restrictions and character availability. If a required character is unavailable, agree a ruling before playing. Use the army-list reference for Arkhan. Pregame rulings.

Civil War: procedure and tables

Before the battle, roll once on the common Civil War table and once on the Vampire Counts table. Ignore the original expansion's Faction rule, as instructed by this campaign.

These results are not merely flavour. Annihilation replaces ordinary length and victory conditions; Hated Rivals includes an explicit simultaneous-death draw. Vampire results can add free units, spells or characteristic changes. Apply the explicit effects of these results to the scenario you choose; do not assume its ordinary duration and victory conditions always remain unchanged.

Night of Ghosts

The fabled Geistnacht falls but once every seven years, when the spirits of the dead rise across the realm to do battle amongst their own.

Roll a D6 for each of your characters and units with the Undead special rule. On a 6, that character or unit gains the Ethereal special rule. If an Ethereal character or unit rolls a 6, however, it loses Ethereal as its members coalesce into solid ectoplasm!

Grasping Graves

The concentration of Dark Magic causes those in shallow graves to reach up and drag the living down among them.

At the end of each Magic phase, the player whose turn it is may nominate one enemy unit. Every model in that unit must pass a Strength test or be slain, with no saves of any kind allowed. A unit can be nominated only once per battle.

On a 4+, the victor takes that item and may use it for the rest of the battle, provided it has not been used up and is otherwise available to that character.

The Red Duke leaves in every outcome

Regardless of the battle result, the Red Duke escapes into the Empire and cannot be used again during The Revolt of Mallobaude. He cannot join a Mousillon army until his return to Bretonnia near the end of the End Times. His lieutenants, if they survived, leave with him under the same restriction.

If the Red Duke loses

He is forced into the service of the Mortarch responsible for Sylvania. He accompanies that Mortarch throughout the defence of the Empire until his eventual return to Bretonnia. If you skipped this interlude, use this outcome and record the lieutenants as dead.

If the Red Duke wins

He persuades other vampires to follow him. From then on, the Mousillon army may contain only one Vampire in addition to Mallobaude for each pair of Necromancers or Master Necromancers, including Arkhan and Kemmler in that count.

His success earns Nagash's recognition and may eventually lead to his appointment as a Mortarch. That is a possible later development, not an immediate promotion or new profile.

On a draw, the Red Duke and his surviving lieutenants still leave. Agree any further consequences; do not apply the victory-specific vampire restriction automatically. No additional Victor's Reward is specified for this interlude.

Return to the campaign stage you paused. Record the Red Duke and surviving lieutenants as unavailable, any deaths, and whether the restricted vampire allowance now applies.
