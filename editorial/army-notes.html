Army Reference Notes

Sources and verification

Campaign overrides come from The Revolt of Mallobaude. Army-book entries were transcribed with the archived BSData WHFB catalogue as an aid. Those entries have not all been checked against the printed books; their listed references should be consulted before play.

Official special-rule definitions are drawn from the WHFB8 Online Rules Index, with individual source references retained. Empty fields indicate missing reference material, not permission to ignore a rule or assume an option is unavailable.

Campaign outcomes

Outcome-dependent commanders and units are listed separately from each army's ordinary choices. Their conditions come from the campaign conclusions. They are not all available together.

Optional four-player army

Morrslieb's Laughing Masses is an additional scenario force for this English edition, not part of the French campaign. It uses the WAP 8th Edition books listed on its army page.
