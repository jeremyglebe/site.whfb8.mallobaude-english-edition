The Siege of Mousillon: sources and rulings

Sources

Campaign narrative, list change, character consequences and conclusion paths: French Guide v1.8 p.27, narrative credited to Thindaraïel. Scenario armies, deployment, duration and victory: Rules v1.5 p.55. The French scenario references the siege rules in Blood in the Badlands; printed pp.88–94 (archive PDF91–97) were visually verified for the preparation, fortress procedures, equipment and scoring consolidated here.

The map printed on Rules p.55 is attributed to Le Duché des Damnés, Warhammer Fantasy Roleplay second edition. It does not give measured deployment zones.

Victory and conclusion conflict

Rules p.55 ends the game immediately when either Mallobaude or Louen dies, declaring his opponent the winner. Otherwise it uses ordinary siege victory conditions.

Guide p.27 independently says the campaign ends in a draw if both Mallobaude and Louen survive. Its conclusion branches also include Loyalists winning while Louen falls, despite the scenario's immediate opposing victory on his death.

Neither source gives an explicit priority rule between those instructions. Do not silently delete one or invent a simultaneous-death sequence. Agree how to distinguish a battlefield victory from the campaign conclusion, including both surviving and both dying, before playing.

Army-list transition

Both Guide p.27 and Rules p.55 require Vampire Counts in place of Mousillon, while the scenario specifically retains Vampire Mallobaude and a Black Grail Knight unit. Arkhan, Kemmler and Krell are prohibited. The prior campaign's Mousillon-specific bonuses should not be silently relabelled as Vampire Counts bonuses; any disputed carryover should be resolved explicitly.

Relief and equipment rulings

Badlands requires a reserve relief force, while Rules55 deploys the entire Rebel army. Agree whether to retain relief before selecting forces. Its printed instant-win deadline is the end of turn five; the fan scenario's six-turn duration does not explicitly extend it.

If buying the affected equipment, agree how machine-independent Undermines and Sally Forth dice choose targets and measure range/line of sight, how the Witches Cauldron selects a random characteristic, and when Boiling Oil activates. These details are not specified by the cited rules.

The scenario overrides starvation, duration, deployment, first turn and leader-death victory. Guide26's necromancer desertions continue after deployment; army-list changes do not remove that general instruction.
