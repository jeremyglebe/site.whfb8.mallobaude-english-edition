The Flight of the Serpent · Editorial notes

Source references, unresolved rules and adaptation decisions.

The narrative assumes Mallobaude survived Quenelles. If your result left him unavailable, the source gives no replacement leader or resurrection procedure here. Do not revive him merely to match the story.

Keep the two steps in this order: Bretonnian defections happen before deployment; necromancer desertions happen after deployment. The source gives no further bespoke instructions for casualty-triggered effects or for distinguishing Necromancers from Master Necromancers in this sentence. Do not silently waive normal consequences or invent a replacement.

Sources and editorial status

Stage procedure, narrative and Unreliable Allies: French Guide v1.8 p.26, credited to Thindaraïel. Entry and branch-specific aftermath: Guide p.21; post-Revelation army changes: pp.18 and 20. Rules p.6 defines the alternative branch's modifiers but does not independently grant them here. Core procedure uses the core rulebook printed pp.142–144 and official update v1.8, PDF pp.2 and6.

This is a consolidated adaptation, distinct from the faithful translation. Character profiles and army lists remain external dependencies; optional skipping does not erase the mandatory campaign changes.

Additional adaptation notes

In the source's account, Mallobaude survives and retreats to his stronghold. Beneath Mousillon wait creatures drawn there by centuries of corruption; he intends to unleash them on his pursuers. But defeat has shaken the dukes who joined his bid for the throne. Each day brings new desertions.

Apply the defeat-related changes before deciding whether to skip this stage. Once they are in effect, the pursuit battles are optional. If skipped, count Louen as having won one victory and proceed to The Siege of Mousillon. The source does not assign a particular defeated duchy, captured duke or reward to that skipped victory.

Use the updated duke record before rolling: a duke captured in an earlier pursuit battle changes the threshold for that duchy's units in later battles. The source does not separately define how embedded characters or deployment placement are handled when a unit changes sides; agree those details rather than adding a silent rule.

The opposing sides use the post-Revelation Mousillon army and Bretonnia. The Asrai have withdrawn; Bretonnian Civil War is no longer used. Preserve character availability, earned rewards, unit origins and any Red Duke interlude restriction.

Kingdom Without a King and Plentiful Ranks do not arise merely from entering this stage. Guide p.21 grants those campaign consequences after Mallobaude wins Quenelles, whereas this route follows Louen's victory. Do not copy the Road to Couronne's −1 Leadership for Break tests or additional Zombie units into this branch without an independently established reason.

The source supplies no special points ratio, deployment map or first-turn exception here. The Battleline setup is an explicit editorial interpretation of its generic Pitched Battle instruction. Apply the pre-deployment and post-deployment defection steps at their stated times.

The source gives no extra campaign consequence for a draw. Record rewards, duke availability, unit defections and necromancer losses, then repeat the pursuit unless Mousillon has been selected.
