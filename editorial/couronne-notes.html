Couronne · Editorial notes

Sources

Scenario: French Rules v1.5 p.54, by Ilmarith. Campaign approach and ending: French Guide v1.8 p.25, with narrative credited to Warhammer: Nagash. Entry route: Guide p.22. Persistent kingless-route effects: Guide p.21 and Rules p.6.

Ordinary scoring is the previously verified core rulebook procedure, printed pp.142–144, incorporating official update v1.8 PDF pp.2 and 6. Its corrected 100-point victory threshold is retained.

Both duelists survive

Rules p.54 says to count ordinary victory points if neither duelist has died after six turns, awarding a tie to the Rebels. Guide p.25 instead ends the campaign in a draw when both survive and asks players to write their own conclusion.

These can produce different campaign outcomes. Agree in advance whether to follow the guide's survival-based campaign ending, the scenario's points outcome, or an explicitly recorded distinction between battle result and campaign result. The consolidated entry does not silently choose a reconciliation.

The phrase “en cas d'égalité” also does not distinguish an exact victory-point tie from an ordinary drawn result below the corrected 100-point threshold. Decide how to apply the Rebel tie-break in advance.

Simultaneous deaths

The immediate-victory clause assumes one duelist dies and the other wins. It supplies no simultaneous-death rule. The separate campaign consequences for each character's death remain stated, but a winner is not invented.

Siege preparation

The source says only to arrange the table “as for a siege” and deploy in the usual order. It names no siege supplement, supplies no map, and gives no wall geometry, gate positions or measured deployment zones. Players must agree a siege setup and rules before play. The entry does not import Blood in the Badlands wholesale or manufacture a map.

The duel starts at the centre in base contact; ensure the agreed siege terrain accommodates this. The source does not specify timing for any other model-placement effects of the Green Knight's army-book rules, so scenario placement must be considered when preparing his profile.

Army accounting and persistent effects

The Rebel budget is the agreed value plus 50%; no reinterpretation as “50% more than the Loyalists after all additions” is added. Plentiful Ranks permits Zombie units but does not explicitly settle their payment or army-selection accounting. Preserve the group's ruling instead of silently calling the units free.

Kingdom Without a King and Plentiful Ranks apply on the route to Couronne following Mallobaude's Quenelles victory. They are not general modifiers for every campaign branch. No additional compulsory duke, war machine or troop type is imposed by this stage.

The scenario requires the Green Knight from the Bretonnian army book, not a later crowned-Gilles profile. Mallobaude uses his vampire profile, not the mortal Black Knight.
