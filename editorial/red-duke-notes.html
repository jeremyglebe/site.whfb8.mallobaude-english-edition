The Red Duke: sources and rulings

Pregame rulings

The chosen scenario supplies points, terrain, deployment and victory conditions. Guide p.19 supplies no replacement for an unavailable required character, a special Arkhan profile, a draw-specific vampire allowance or an explicit Victor's Reward. The Red Duke and surviving lieutenants leave regardless of result.

Sources and limits

Timing, optional skip, narrative and consequences: French Guide v1.8 p.19, whose narrative is credited as a translation from Josh Reynolds's blog. Revelation's army-list change is on p.18.

Civil War procedure and tables: Rules v1.5 pp.7–8 and 10. The Red Duke's profile is on p.30; his lieutenants are on p.31. Full army entries remain linked dependencies.

This is a consolidated adaptation, separate from the faithful translation. It keeps the source's open scenario choice rather than inventing a canonical battle, map or ending.
