La Maisontaal: sources and rulings

Agree before playing

  • Character placement and General rules: the fan requires Arkhan/Kemmler at the abbey and Mallobaude/Krell at Last Charge, but does not expressly remove the official conditional character requirements or rewrite Krell's General rule, Arkhan's free-character/General/no-casting rules, or Theodoric's Arkhan-specific Hatred.
  • Arkhan's version: Mousillon references the Tomb Kings Arkhan, while official Last Charge specifies the Mortarch of Sacrament. The fan does not resolve this difference.
  • Resurrection and living troops: Dark Enchantments says all attacking units recover Wounds, while its referenced procedure concerns Undead. Mousillon can contain living troops.
  • Transfers: agree entry edges and any casualty-handling interpretation; no Quenelles transfer procedure applies.
  • Route to the duel: Guide p.23 sends a Rebel winner to Alakanash, whereas p.24 says the duel occurs only if Arkhan forced the abbey doors. A victory-point or combined victory need not satisfy that literal requirement.
  • Unprovided campaign states: no draw route is stated, and required characters may become casualties before the duel. Do not revive them or apply a new route without agreement.

These are source gaps, not new rules. Keep agreed rulings separate from the faithful translation.
