Editorial reference

Decisions and Review Notes

Battle sources and rules questions · Army-reference notes

Agreed approach

HTML/CSS is the master edition. PDF will be an export, not the primary editable file.
Translate meaning, not French syntax. The English should read like an English-language Warhammer supplement while preserving the source's claims and tone.
Canonical English names override French localisation. This affects titles, characters, units, ranks, and places.

Decision-recording protocol

Every substantive intervention receives a ledger entry. Named terminology, inferred meaning, canon reconciliation, rules interpretation, source correction, and any rewrite that changes emphasis or specificity are recorded individually.
Mechanical copy-editing is recorded in bounded batches. Grammar, punctuation, British spelling, standard WFB8 capitalisation, and removal of literal French syntax may be grouped only when the affected book and page range are stated. A batch entry never covers a change to rules meaning.
Unresolved questions remain visible. A passage is marked as queued rather than silently settled whenever more than one materially different reading remains plausible.

Second-pass ledger

ED-001 - Front-matter prose, Guide pages 3-7 and Rules pages 2-7.

Category: bounded copy-editing batch. The second pass removes translation artefacts, regularises British English, and restores natural English sentence structure while preserving the source's claims, credits, disclaimers, and tone.

ED-002 - “Not complete” is rendered as “not self-contained”.

The French n'est pas complet explains that each volume depends upon its companion, not that the project is an unfinished draft. “Not self-contained” communicates that intended relationship without falsely describing the published version as incomplete.

ED-003 - Canonical English publication titles replace literal French titles.

Duché des Damnés is Barony of the Damned, and Bain de Sang dans les Terres Arides is Blood in the Badlands. Edition numbers use normal English ordinals.

ED-004 - Rules prose uses the vocabulary of English WFB8.

Page 6 uses Wounds rather than “Life Points”, D6 rather than “a die”, Leadership modifiers rather than literal characteristic phrasing, and the Fay Enchantress rather than “Morgiana the Fay”. The spell's omitted statement that mounts are unaffected has been restored from the French source.

ED-005 - The page 6 spell quotation is rewritten idiomatically.

Dispersant des fragments gâchés de vertu et de noblesse is not intelligible as the literal “scattering wasted fragments”. The English conveys the image as casting aside the tattered remnants of virtue and nobility before calling down the Lady's wrath; the rules effect remains unchanged.

ED-006 - Official Civil War wording is restored on Rules pages 8 and 10.

Category: source restoration. The general and Vampire Counts tables were translated into French from Jervis Johnson's English Warhammer: Civil War article and then translated back in the first English pass. Their original English headings and WFB8 terms have now been recovered from the English article. This restores names including “Pent-Up Fury”, “Undeserving”, “Grasping Graves”, “Lahmia Ascendant”, “Pallid Handmaidens”, and “Master Strike”. Where the project omitted or lightly altered connective prose, the English has been edited for fit without changing the rules effect.

ED-007 - The Bretonnian Civil War table is project-original material.

The table on Rules page 9 is not a lost Games Workshop English table. The surviving WarFo development discussion shows the project authors composing and revising it in French. Its English headings are therefore editorial translations rather than recovered canon: “Favours of the Lady”, “Thoroughbreds”, “As a Token of My Esteem”, “Valour”, “The Jacquerie”, and “Lances of Heroes”. “Ancient Enmity” is restored from the official table because that row was copied from Jervis Johnson's rules.

ED-008 - Rules page 11 uses idiomatic campaign-reward names and established Vampire terminology.

Ordre venu d'en Haut is “Command from On High”, Invocation Nocturne is “Nocturnal Summoning”, and Focus de Mort is “Nexus of Death”. Vampire Nouveau-né is the established English rank “Vampire Thrall”, not the literal “Newborn Vampire”. The source's undefined 1+1D3 cavaliers remains “1+D3 cavalry models” rather than silently restricting the reward to Black Knights or another unit.

ED-009 - Civil War rules and rewards, Rules pages 7-11.

Category: bounded copy-editing batch. Sentence structure, British spelling, capitalisation, dice notation, tests, saves, challenges, special rules, and unit names are normalised to English WFB8 usage. Mechanical quantities, timing, target restrictions, and victory effects are preserved except where a separate ledger entry explicitly records a source restoration or interpretation.

Editorial interventions already made

Massif d'Orquemont became Massif Orcal. The French name appears in the Guide, but the English source passage from Barony of the Damned uses Massif Orcal.
Landouin and Thierry became Landuin and Thierulf. These are the established English spellings for Gilles's Companions.
“Chevaliers sans âme” became “honourless knights.” “Soulless knights” is possible literally, but the passage describes the moral quality of Mallobaude's living supporters rather than an undead troop type.
The awkward rumour sentence on source page 8 was repaired. The French obscures the sense. The English source behind it says Mallobaude allowed flattering stories of his Questing Knight deeds to circulate among the nobles of Mousillon.
“Lost Duchy” remains a fixed title. Bretonnian territories are normally called Dukedoms in the English edition, but Barony of the Damned names Mousillon “the Lost Duchy”.
The French localisation note on page 14 is retained as historical context. It is labelled “French edition note” so an English reader does not mistake it for an instruction about this edition.
The campaign map has been semantically rebuilt. Its arrows were checked against all twelve translated stages: the two Quenelles branches now lead to the Road to Couronne and the Serpent's Flight respectively, the La Maisontaal detour returns correctly, and the repeating conquest and pursuit stages loop to themselves.

First review choices

King and Royarch.

House style: use “King of Bretonnia” in headings and normal prose, but retain “Royarch” when the text explicitly invokes the formal End Times title.

The 2523 date.

The Guide's 2523 date is retained. English summaries also place the Battle of Quenelles and the final years of the civil war in 2523, so no silent chronological correction is warranted.

Alliance Against Undeath.

This is the agreed name for Alliance contre la Non-Vie. It retains the abstract sense of the French and reads as a suitably Warhammer-style formal army name.

Mallobaude's former restraint is preserved as a matter of chronology.

The prohibition against executing fellow nobles comes from Barony of the Damned. The executions on pages 14 and 27 are fan-campaign outcomes. Page 10 therefore says that for many years Mallobaude remained honour-bound and had never ordered such an execution; the later branches depict the eventual collapse of that restraint without presenting those events as Games Workshop canon.

Chevaliers-Moires remains the unit's proper name.

The French campaign authors developed the unit from official references to the mirror-lake's spirit-knights, but Chevaliers-Moires is their novel unit name. The proper name is retained and the relationship to the spirit-knights is explained in its first substantial English description.

Sacremor remains untranslated.

The title appears unchanged in English accounts of the End Times. It is introduced as “Sacremor, the Green Knight” where context requires.

WFB8 Rules first pass

Rules language follows English WFB8 terminology.

Literal renderings such as “invulnerable save,” “Command,” “Life Point,” “Fiasco,” and “Cabbalistic Object” have been normalised to ward save, Leadership, Wound, miscast, and Arcane Item. Army-book unit names likewise take priority over descriptive translations.

The Wildwood Host keeps the official English names of its imported Sylvaneth concepts.

The fan rules adapt Age of Sigmar material to WFB8. Branchwych, Bittergrub, Kurnoth Hunters, Quiverlings, and Spite-Revenants therefore use their published English names rather than newly literal translations.

The Green Knight's established English wargear names are used.

The French names are rendered as the Dolorous Blade, Shadow Steed, Silver Lance of the Blessed, and Armour of the Green Knight. These choices are shared by Gilles, Sacremor, and the Chevaliers-Moires where relevant.

Collaborative policy and rules decisions

ED-010 - The fan campaign's continuity is translated as written.

Canonical English sources are used to recover established names and references, not to rewrite the campaign into Games Workshop's official sequence of events. Campaign-specific deaths, survival, allegiances, and alternate outcomes remain as the source presents them, without added continuity warnings or “Campaign Fate” labels.

ED-011 - The Battle of Quenelles bonus is a flat 1" of additional movement.

The French un Pas de mouvement supplémentaire names a unit of tabletop distance, not an additional movement action or an increase to the Movement characteristic. Louen and Mallobaude therefore add 1" to the distance of a normal move, march or charge made directly towards the other by the shortest possible route.

ED-012 - Earlier ward-save interpretation (superseded for translation by ED-024).

The curse is the counterpart to the Lady's Blessing, which grants a ward save on a 6+, improving to 5+ against Strength 5 or higher. Accordingly, each Black Grail Knight that passes a ward save rolls one D6 and is slain on a 6+, or on a 5+ if the attack is Strength 5 or higher. Armour saves and Regeneration do not trigger the curse.

ED-013 - The Banshee uses the Lore of Death or the Lore of the Vampires.

The French rule names la Magie de la Mort ou la Nécromancie. Its development notes explain that the Banshee's spellcasting was inherited from her 5th-edition profile, where Necromancy was an older magic category. For WFB8, it is therefore normalised to the Lore of the Vampires rather than the End Times Lore of Undeath, whose French name is Domaine de la Non-vie.

ED-014 - The page 41 Crusade Army belongs to Conclusions I and II, with a different commander in each.

In Conclusion I, Gilles becomes King and declares the crusade, but Louen the High Paladin commands the expedition to the Empire. In Conclusion II, Louen remains King and cannot join it, so Sacremor, the Green Knight, commands it and the Chevaliers-Moires explicitly accompany him. Conclusion III's “Crusaders of Despair” are a separate narrative force: the rules represent its survivors as Bretonnian mercenary allies in an Order army under Louen the Dispossessed, not with the page 41 Crusade roster. The army reference records these branches separately and does not present Gilles as a Crusade commander.

ED-015 - Campaign army references distinguish printed entries, conditional additions, and external army-book data.

Each reference preserves the campaign supplement's printed roster as its primary list. Profiles unlocked by a conclusion are shown in a separate outcome-dependent section rather than silently inserted. Where the supplement delegates a unit to another army book, archived WHFB roster data is used only as a transcription aid; campaign-specific rules override it, and unavailable fields remain marked “Not specified in this supplement” until checked against the printed book.

ED-016 - Army and unit special rules are expandable reference text, not name-only cross-references.

The Campaign Armies pages serve a different purpose from the typeset Rules booklet and may deliberately repeat its material. Army-specific rules therefore open to the complete campaign effect, while each unit special-rule name opens to its available definition. Campaign-original text links to the translated Rules booklet. Official WFB8 definitions are stored locally from the WHFB8 Online Rules Index and retain a direct source link. If no reliable wording is available, the disclosure remains unfilled apart from a search link; no rule is inferred.

ED-017 - A campaign variant inherits the source unit’s normal special rules unless the supplement replaces one.

Several campaign profiles were constructed from an army-book unit but initially carried only their new campaign additions in the roster data. The reference now preserves the inherited rules before applying the campaign changes: the Banshee retains the Tomb Banshee rules before adding Hover and White Witch; the Dark Knight retains the Wight King rules; Black Grail Knights retain the Blood Knight rules; the Red Duke retains the Vampire Lord rules; Coeddil and the Ancient Treekin retain their source forest-spirit rules; and Spite-Revenants retain the Spirit Host rules with Undead replaced by Forest Spirit as the supplement instructs.

French-source fidelity review, 8 November 2026

ED-018 - The opening scenario's fortress is optional, not a compulsory deployment location.

Category: translation correction. Rules page 46 says la forteresse placée dans la zone Bretonnienne est optionnelle: the fortress in the Bretonnian deployment zone may be omitted if unavailable. The English diagram note incorrectly placed the Bretonnian force inside it. The note now follows the French. Page 45's actual deployment instructions remain unchanged: war machines must deploy inside the fortress, missile units may do so, and all other units and Mallobaude deploy outside.

ED-019 - Conclusion II retains the duration of the renegade Dukes' removal.

Category: omitted rule restored. Guide page 30 expressly states that executed or banished Dukes may not be played for the entire End Times. The English now includes that duration and the option to replace them with unnamed Bretonnian Lords, rather than only describing their execution or exile.

ED-020 - La Maisontaal protects the crypt; Arkhan seeks its artefact.

Category: mistranslated pronoun corrected. In Rules page 52, La crypte qu'il protège refers to the fortified monastery. The former English “The crypt he protects” made Arkhan appear to protect the crypt he is attacking. “Its crypt contains an ancient artefact that Arkhan has long sought” preserves the intended relationship.

ED-021 - Two narrative details restored in the Guide.

Category: source fidelity. On page 12, une dizaine de lances bénies is “some ten blessed lances”, not a dozen. On page 28, en l'absence d'héritier means there is no heir to succeed Louen; the English again states this cause of the succession dispute instead of merely referring to an empty throne.

ED-022 - Quenelles reinforcement prose, Rules page 50.

Category: bounded copy-editing batch. Removed a duplicated “for the Loyalist player” in the recommendation to send Orion or Ariel as reinforcements. The reinforcement allowance and all mechanical effects are unchanged.

ED-023 - Remaining-page source-fidelity pass.

Rules pp6–11 restore the French restrictions and optional choices, including Break tests, captured standards, Undead-only Night Ghosts, slaying in challenges, and free Blood Knights. Rules pp26–27 restore the explicit baseline units; p34 restores the White Elm Wand's Creative Cycle restriction; p37 restores Forest Strider; p43 grants immunity to Panic, not all Psychology. Guide and Rules front/back matter omissions are restored. See translation-review.md for coverage, remaining corrections and source issues. No new balance changes are intended.

ED-024 - Source ambiguity remains in the translation.

Under the source-faithful translation policy, Rules p24 again says “save”, matching the French's unspecified sauvegarde. ED-012's ward-save-only reading is an editorial interpretation, not the wording of the French source; it must not be presented as literal translation. Other French errors and prior silent corrections are tracked separately in translation-review.md.

ED-025 - French errors and English corrections are separate.

Rules p21 preserves the French reference to page23; the correct destination appears to be page24. Rules p32 preserves the French's repeated “Special Units” heading; “Rare Units” is the apparent intended correction. These are identified here rather than silently substituted in the translation. The remaining-page fidelity corrections and their status are recorded in translation-review.md.

ED-026 - The Crown's Panic protection uses explicit test wording.

At Jeremy's request, the English expresses the French Panic protection as: “A unit with line of sight to the King automatically passes any Panic tests it is required to take.” This avoids presenting “Immune to Panic” as a named special rule. It does not grant Immune to Psychology. The separate 18-inch Leadership benefit is unchanged; no range limit is added to the line-of-sight Panic protection.

ED-027 - Army cards apply the campaign overrides.

The Rebel Sorcerers use the four lores, warhorse-only mounts and casting/miscast rules on p21. Mortal Rebel Black Grail Knights use Grail Knights with the Black Grail Vow, not the later Blood Knights upgrade. Arkhan's p23 Master Necromancer status, Undead replacement, alternative lores and free Master of the Dead replace conflicting army-book fields; the source does not say he cannot be General. Vampire Lords are restricted to Mallobaude's final victory. Ancient Treekin use only the Elder profile and 3+ Scaly Skin. Both post-campaign Louens lose the King's Army rule. Missing Mallobaude and Rage profiles and the Blood Dragon powers are restored from pp19–20. The Black Grail curse disclosure follows ED-024's unspecified “save”, not the superseded ward-only interpretation. Arkhan’s inherited Fly flag is removed: flight belongs to his unavailable chariot; his Tomb Blade, Liber Mortis and Staff of Nagash are retained (cross-checked against the WFB8 index). Rebel Sorcerer magic items are paid options, not free; unverified allowances and incomplete mounted profiles remain explicitly referred to the army books. These are reference corrections, not new balance rules; inherited source contradictions remain documented separately.
