Alakanash · Editorial notes

Source references, unresolved rules and adaptation decisions.

The narrative places the staff in Kemmler's hands, but the playable scenario uses three chests and a search. Do not start him carrying Alakanash: follow the scenario victory procedure. Its introduction identifies Tzeentch's purpose as stealing the staff to obstruct Nagash's return.

The source provides no diagram, exact door position, debris classification or precise measurement method for the comparative chest-distance instruction. Agree these before deployment; this entry does not invent dimensions or terrain effects.

The search plainly seeks one staff, but the source does not specify how later chest rolls work after it has been found. It also does not define “reaching” a chest or the door, nor who may take or use the secondary magic items. The ring's French name is retained because its correspondence to an English item has not been independently verified. No item powers have been guessed.

An objective win with both leaders alive, a draw, or simultaneous deaths has no matching campaign-resolution branch here. Do not apply the death consequences merely because one player won. The guide does not state a Victor's Reward for this episode.

Sources and editorial status

Scenario: French Rules v1.5 p.53, Duel de Nécromanciens, by Thindaraïel with Dreadaxe and Ilmarith. Context and death-triggered consequences: French Guide v1.8 pp.23–24; its narrative is credited to Warhammer: Nagash.

Civil War procedure and tables: Rules pp.7–8 and 10, drawn from the corrected English translation. This is a consolidated adaptation, distinct from the faithful translated documents. Full army and character rules remain external dependencies; unprinted outcomes and item effects have not been invented.

Additional adaptation notes

Guide p.23 sends a Rebel victory at La Maisontaal to this stage. Page 24 also says this episode occurs only if Arkhan has forced the abbey doors. Those conditions are not necessarily identical: an ordinary victory need not mean that the abbey was entered. If they diverge in your game, agree the interpretation rather than inventing a forced result.

Bring forward which characters survived the abbey battle. The source does not explain how to stage this duel if Arkhan or Kemmler has already become unavailable.

In the source's account, Arkhan notices Kemmler's absence as the abbey's defences collapse. The trail leads through scorched corpses to the crypt, where broken seals and dead knights mark the Lichemeister's passage. Kemmler turns on his former ally with Alakanash in his hands.

The source does not specify which version of Arkhan to use or expressly settle character-percentage accounting for the free leaders. Agree those details before building armies.

Apply Civil War (Vampire Counts), using the procedure and tables. Patrols rules are optional, not required; they are not incorporated into this default setup. The scenario names no Death on the Winds or Undeath Ascendant rule, so those End Times modifiers have not been silently imported.

Only Arkhan or Kemmler may carry Alakanash. The character must personally reach the chest containing it to pick it up. He may then move while carrying it. The scenario grants the staff no additional combat or spell powers.

Apply any Civil War result that explicitly overrides these conditions. The source does not resolve Alakanash being discovered but unclaimed when the game ends. Nor does the baseline rule resolve simultaneous deaths; an applicable table result may address them.
